Access

Your choice of access.

Web apps get HTTPS on your private mesh. Trust your cluster’s certificate authority once per device; app certificates renew automatically. Open individual apps to your LAN when you choose.

Updates

Know what you’re trying.

Rasputin is alpha software. Image layouts and update formats can change. Try it on a lab network before putting it in front of a network you depend on.

IPv4 only. Intrusion detection observes traffic; it does not block it. Managed backups exclude media libraries. Keep your backup recovery code outside the cluster.
